Privacy Policy

Cloud Motion Lab · Last updated April 28, 2026

Stud Finder Pro is built to do its job offline and to leave you alone afterward. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not collect.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The app does not require an account. It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising purposes.

How the magnetometer is used

Stud Finder Pro reads the magnetic-field sensor (magnetometer) inside your iPhone to detect ferromagnetic metal behind walls. These readings stay on your device. They are not transmitted, stored on a server, or shared with any third party.

Anonymous analytics

The app uses TelemetryDeck for anonymous, privacy-preserving usage analytics. TelemetryDeck collects coarse, non-identifying signals (such as which screen was viewed, whether a scan was started, app version, device type, country) hashed with a daily rotating salt. No persistent device identifier is stored. You can read TelemetryDeck's privacy approach at telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Subscription and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. Stud Finder Pro receives only an Apple-issued receipt confirming entitlement. We do not see your Apple ID, your payment details, or your billing address. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your Apple ID at the top, then tap Subscriptions.

What we do not do

  • No third-party advertising networks
  • No tracking across apps or websites (we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track)
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any data
  • No background location, no contacts, no microphone, no camera

Children

Stud Finder Pro is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. It is suitable for children but is designed as a tool for general use, not specifically for children, and complies with COPPA by collecting no personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, the "Last updated" date above will move and we will note the change in the app's What's New on its next release.
