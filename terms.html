What the app does

Stud Finder Pro reads your iPhone's magnetometer to detect ferromagnetic metal (nails, screws, metal studs, pipes) behind walls. The app provides a real-time signal-strength readout, audio feedback, and haptic alerts to help you locate metal fasteners.

What the app cannot do

The magnetometer detects ferromagnetic metals only. It cannot detect:

  • Wood directly (we infer studs by locating the nails or screws fastening drywall to them)
  • Plastic pipes
  • Live electrical wiring (we may pick up the metal sheath, not the current)
  • Non-magnetic metals such as copper or aluminum

The accuracy of any reading depends on wall material, the strength of nearby magnetic sources, and whether the iPhone is held flat against the wall with no case.

Use at your own risk

Stud Finder Pro is a consumer assistant, not a certified construction-grade detection instrument. Always use independent verification — a small pilot hole, a calibrated commercial stud finder, or a professional contractor — before drilling into a wall.
